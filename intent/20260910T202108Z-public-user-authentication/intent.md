# Intent: public user authentication
Author: Patricio Mac Adden. Timestamp: 2026-09-10T20:21:08Z. Status: accepted.

## Problem
The application has no pages or user accounts, so people cannot establish an individual identity or securely access future protected features.

## Proposed outcome
Provide public account registration and email/password sign-in so people can securely access the application. Create a homepage that shows authenticated people “Signed in as...” with a sign-out control, while visitors see sign-in and sign-up controls.

## Affected users and systems
Public end users and the application’s homepage and account-access experience.

## Constraints
The initial release supports email/password authentication, public self-registration, sign-up, sign-in, sign-out, and password reset. Roles, permissions, and multi-factor authentication are out of scope.

## Open questions
The future pages or actions requiring authentication are to be decided during design.
